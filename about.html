An ode to the little me

Over the New Moon came to me when I was actively seeking to heal, and when I realized that healing had been finding me for years without my knowing.

One thing that bothered me deeply for a long time: why did my trauma feel heavier as I grew older, as I understood more? Shouldn't understanding make things lighter? I thought awareness was supposed to be the cure. Instead, it cracked things open further.

What I eventually found was this: the weight doesn't grow. You do. And when you grow, you finally become big enough to hold what was always there.

This is my way of sitting with that: traveling, creating, cooking, pampering myself, dressing up, feeling every emotion fully, and figuring out what it means to actually live while you heal. Not a wellness destination. Not a before-and-after. Just a real person in the middle of it.

The name comes from the new moon, the darkest phase, the one we tend to skip past. It always follows the full moon, the brightness, the peak. And then comes the dark, and then the beginning again. Something always starts there, quietly, before anyone can see it.

A few things about me

Where are you right now, in life?

In a safe space, surrounded by people who make me feel safe. That alone feels like everything.

Something you used to believe that you don't anymore?

That everything happens for a reason. For the longest time I believed that, so I'd blame myself for what happened, telling myself it was something I was meant to learn. But sometimes, some things just happen. And that's allowed too.

What do you do when things feel heavy?

I go surf. I cook. I clean. I hike. I find my way back to something bigger than whatever's going on inside my head, nature, usually, or learning something new from scratch.

What are you most excited about right now?

Continuing to heal. I didn't expect healing to feel exciting, but it does.

Who is this blog for?

You who are healing. You who want to heal. You who aren't ready to heal yet. And the little you inside of you. The one who just needs to know someone else is in it too.

What you'll find here

Healing

The slow, honest work of understanding yourself. No shortcuts, no performance of wellness.

Adventure

Travel, movement, being outdoors. The places and moments that remind you you're alive.

Creating

Making things as a form of processing. Cooking, writing, building, art. Whatever it takes to get it out.
